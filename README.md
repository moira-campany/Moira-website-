# Moira-website-
MOIRA is an innovative manufacturing company based in Burundi, dedicated to producing high-quality products that improve everyday life. Our mission is to deliver durable, affordable, and sustainable solutions while promoting local innovation and economic development
MOIRA Brand Identity (Traditional English Version)

1. Brand Name

MOIRA

2. Slogan

Made in Burundi, Trusted Everywhere.

3. Vision

To become a leading Burundian company recognized for creating and manufacturing high-quality products that compete successfully in regional and international markets.

4. Mission

To use research, innovation, and locally available resources to develop high-quality, durable products that improve everyday life while promoting sustainable development in Burundi.

5. Core Values

- Innovation – We embrace creativity and continuous improvement.
- Quality – We are committed to excellence in every product we create.
- Integrity – We conduct our business with honesty, transparency, and responsibility.
- Sustainability – We value environmentally responsible practices and the efficient use of natural resources.
- Customer First – We place our customers at the center of everything we do.

6. Official Brand Colors

- 🟤 Earth Brown — #6B4226 (Clay, heritage, and strength)
- 🟢 Forest Green — #2E6B3A (Growth, sustainability, and nature)
- ⚪ Ivory White — #F7F4ED (Purity, simplicity, and professionalism)
- ⚫ Charcoal Black — #2C2C2C (Confidence, elegance, and readability)
- 🟠 Terracotta — #C96A3D (Ceramics, craftsmanship, and warmth)

7. Typography

Logo Font

- Cinzel or Playfair Display

Body Text

- Poppins or Montserrat

8. Logo Usage Guidelines

- Use the full-color logo on a white or light background.
- Use the black version when color printing is not available.
- Use the white logo on dark backgrounds.
- Do not stretch, distort, rotate, or change the logo colors.

9. Brand Communication & Social Media Style

Every publication should include:

- The MOIRA logo.
- Official brand colors only.
- High-quality, clean photography.
- The slogan: Made in Burundi, Trusted Everywhere.
- Company contact information.

10. Brand Voice

MOIRA communicates with:

- Professionalism
- Clarity
- Trustworthiness
- Innovation
- Respect

11. Content Strategy

MOIRA shares content about:

- Research and product development.
- Manufacturing processes.
- Customer stories and testimonials.
- Innovation and new ideas.
- New product launches.
- Company news and achievements.

12. Packaging Guidelines

- Clean white boxes or packaging.
- MOIRA logo displayed prominently.
- Earth Brown and Forest Green as the primary brand colors.
- Slogan printed clearly:

Made in Burundi, Trusted Everywhere.

Brand Promise

MOIRA is committed to delivering innovative, high-quality, and sustainable products proudly made in Burundi and trusted around the world.
